Support Pilot: De-escalation Skies

An interactive, gamified flight simulator built for customer-facing engineers to practice active listening and customer de-escalation in a high-stakes, zero-risk sandbox environment.

 How to Play
1. **Take Off:** Click the "TAKE OFF" button on the start screen.
2. **Steer Your Aircraft:** Use your keyboard **Up / Down Arrow Keys** or the **W / S** keys to navigate your jet.
3. **Hit the Target (Gain Points):** Steer your plane directly into **Green Banners** containing active-listening support statements.
4. **Dodge the Danger (Avoid Damage):** Completely avoid and dodge **Red Banners** containing toxic, dismissive, or bad tech-support phrases.
5. **Win Condition:** Keep your aircraft integrity alive and score as many points as possible!

Tech Stack & Lab Features
- **Frontend:** Pure HTML5, CSS3 transitions, and Vanilla JavaScript (No external frameworks or heavy assets required).
- **Physical Lab Integration:** Designed for dual-screen setups, projectors, or tactile controllers inside our AI Learning Lab.
- **Purpose:** Built to demonstrate how gamification can lower learning friction and turn stressful customer service scenarios into routine, fun training routines.
